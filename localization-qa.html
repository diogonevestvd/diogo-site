01 / Brief

A small test structured like a real QA workflow.

The scenario simulates an international SaaS product preparing an onboarding flow for Portugal. The goal is not to show a long translation, but to make linguistic judgment visible: what is wrong, why it matters and how to correct it without losing the function of the interface copy.

03 / Method

Review does not stop when the grammar is technically correct.

1

Context

Define locale, audience, product, intent and space constraints.

2

Evaluate

Compare source and output for accuracy, fluency, terminology and locale fit.

3

Localize

Rewrite for natural PT-PT while preserving the function of each interface string.

4

Verify

Run a second pass for consistency, punctuation, numbers, currency and terminology.

04 / Deliverable

The client receives clean copy and a concise record of what changed.

For this type of work I would deliver implementation-ready copy plus a short QA report containing the issues that matter. The report makes decisions auditable and helps product or localization teams identify recurring error patterns.
