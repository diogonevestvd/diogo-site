01 / Context

Making scientific information available to Portuguese readers without losing precision.

The collaboration was developed within the Technical and Scientific Translation (English/Portuguese) course in the BA in Translation at NOVA FCSH. The objective was to make scientific information accessible in Portuguese to readers without English proficiency while preserving the technical content and intent of the source text.

02 / Work

Translating for the reader without simplifying the science.

The translation required attention to specialised terminology, conceptual consistency, scientific register and natural European Portuguese. The goal was not simply to replace words, but to produce text that reads naturally in Portuguese without changing the scientific meaning.

03 / QA and review

The first version was not treated as the final version.

The published article passed through several review layers before reaching readers. It received peer review, academic supervision and client review. This process allowed terminology, clarity and domain appropriateness to be challenged before publication.
