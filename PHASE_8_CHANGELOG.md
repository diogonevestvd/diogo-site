# Phase 8 Changelog

## Goal
Make the broader product and business work support the language specialization instead of competing with it, and make English the default site language.

## Changes
- English is now served from the site root and acts as the default language.
- Portuguese moved to `/pt/`; Spanish remains under `/es/`.
- Former `/en/` URLs are retained as compatibility redirects to the English root.
- Canonical URLs, hreflang relationships and language switchers were updated.
- Added `x-default` hreflang pointing to the English version.
- Homepage selected work now separates core language evidence from product and business context.
- NOVA FCSH Translation, MT & QA was promoted into the homepage evidence set.
- Sul was removed from the homepage to reduce competition with stronger language evidence.
- Work archive now has two explicit groups: core language evidence and secondary product/business context.
- Morly now contains a section explaining the product constraints relevant to localization QA without presenting product work as translation experience.
- Bubble B was rewritten around verified operations, customer communication, supplier coordination, team onboarding and commercial execution.
- Sul is explicitly treated as additional product work and now connects information hierarchy to interface language.
- No em dash character is used in public HTML, CSS or this changelog.
