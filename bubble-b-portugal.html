Bubble B Portugal

Communication under real operating constraints.

A customer-facing operation I managed across daily service, stock, suppliers, team onboarding, social content, promotions and community initiatives. It adds commercial and operational context to a language-led portfolio.

Type
Retail / food service
Role
Manager / operations
Period
2024-2025

01 / Operating context

Language and communication have consequences when customers are standing in front of you.

I managed daily operations, stock control, supplier communication and customer service, typically serving around 25 to 60 customers on a normal day. I also helped teach the job to about 10 team members and directly supervised one employee.

02 / Communication

The work moved between operations and customer-facing communication.

I created social posts, promotions, menu changes and community initiatives while coordinating with suppliers and other businesses. The goal was practical: make offers understandable, keep the operation consistent and give people a reason to return.

Customer communicationSupplier coordinationSocial contentPromotions & menusTeam onboarding

03 / What this adds

Commercial context makes language decisions less abstract.

Bubble B is not presented as translation experience. It demonstrates responsibility, direct communication and an understanding that wording, tone and clarity affect customer behavior and business outcomes.

What this demonstrates

Bubble B Portugal adds real operational and client-facing context to the portfolio: responsibility, communication, execution and commercial judgment.
