Language evaluation

A sentence can be correct and still be wrong for the product.

When evaluating MT or AI-generated language, I separate surface correctness from actual language quality. A sentence may be grammatical while still being inaccurate, inconsistent, unnatural or inappropriate for Portugal.

1

Meaning

Compare against the source and identify omissions, additions or shifts in meaning.

2

Locale

Check vocabulary, constructions, formatting and conventions for PT-PT.

3

Naturalness

Remove calques and formulations that read as translated or mechanically generated.

4

Consistency

Verify terminology, tone, punctuation and cross-segment patterns before delivery.
